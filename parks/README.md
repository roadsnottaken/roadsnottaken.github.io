# Parks

Imagine what our parks could look like if we took back our parking lots.

Same format as The Roads Not Taken: one zoomable world (`index.html` + `zoom.js`, no dependencies) plus simple scroll pages.
Built to be served from a subfolder (e.g. roadsnottaken.github.io/parks/); every link is relative.

- `index.html`: zoom view. Regions: hero, the land, wins timeline, 01 who runs which lot, 02 the money, 03 empty asphalt, 04 today/imagine, 05 explore, 06 my story & asks, 07 why now / contacts / sources.
- `alt-a/`, `alt-b/`, `alt-c/`: three lot-conversion options (drawings are illustrative).
- `before/`: the land and the Nations' place names. `list/`: every source, the unverified items, photo credits. `story/`: full long read.
- `img/`: Wikimedia Commons photos (open licences, unedited, credited on the page). `-s` = 640px, `-l` = 1600px.
- Items marked UNVERIFIED could not be confirmed from a primary source.
