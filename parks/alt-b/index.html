Option B · creek & green edge

Give the edge back to water.

Lift the stalls nearest the water and let a creek, wetland and path take their place.

Canyon Creek, Spanish Banks

A buried creek ran under the western lot at Spanish Banks. The project opened it up, “converting 65 parking stalls to greenspace” and creating 0.4 hectares of habitat, with design informed by the Musqueam, Squamish and Tsleil-Waututh Nations. It had a $992,800 federal contribution (City of Vancouver, June 12, 2025).

Ambleside waterfront

Since a mid-1970s policy, West Vancouver has bought 32 Argyle Avenue lots to return the waterfront to park. 1448 Argyle was bought in December 2023 and opened as park in summer 2024; the last lot, 1444 Argyle, was announced on March 31, 2025 (West Vancouver). The 2016 concept plan called for more green spaces and public waterfront access (concept plan).

What stays, what changes

  • Stays: stalls nearest the road and the trailhead.
  • Changes: the waterside rows become creek, wetland, a path and a viewing deck.

The parking we keep

  • Pickup and drop-off spots, so a few minutes at a class or a playground never costs $85.
  • Accessible stalls, closest to the entrance.
  • Trailhead parking, sized to real peak counts.
  • Free short stops: nine in ten Ambleside stays are two hours or less (West Vancouver).
