The evidence

Figures as published. Ranges stay ranges, and estimates are marked as estimates. Each one links to its source.

Disease, displacement and the reserve system cut these numbers sharply after contact. The Nations are still here: the Musqueam, Squamish and Tsleil-Waututh Nations live and govern in this territory today.

Data

    Inspiration

    Bruce Macdonald, Vancouver: A Visual History (Talonbooks, 1992). We followed his idea of a land-and-water-use map for the 1850s, but not his drawing. Every site here comes from the sources listed for it.

    Software

    three.js r160 (MIT licence), served from this site.

    Caveats: the shoreline is Proctor's 1880–1920 reconstruction, the best open dataset we found. By the 1850s it would have been the same or wider. The hills are today's elevation. The dots mark general areas, not exact sites. Blank areas were not empty land.
