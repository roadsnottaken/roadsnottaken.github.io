Vancouverites stopped the freeways that would have cut through their city's core. That choice made the city we know, and the same kind of choice is in front of us now.

The evidence

500+

people packed city hall on November 23, 1967. The Chinatown route was dropped in 1968.

21,000+

petition signatures against the third crossing by February 1972.

28 km

of uninterrupted seawall today, on waterfront the freeway would have taken.

1

piece of the freeway plan was ever built: the Georgia and Dunsmuir viaducts. Removal was approved in 2015, and the City says the timeline "is still uncertain."
