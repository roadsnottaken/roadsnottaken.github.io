What if the freeway had been built?

Vancouverites stopped the freeways that would have cut through their city's core. That choice made the city we know, and the same kind of choice is in front of us now.

What's at stake now

  • The viaduct lands. Removal was approved in 2015 but isn't funded or scheduled. Plans call for parks, about 1,800 social housing units and redress at Hogan's Alley.
  • Crossing the inlet. The debate is back, but this time it's about trains and buses, not lanes into downtown.
  • The shoreline. The City plans for about 1 m of sea-level rise by 2100.
